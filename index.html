About

A one-person music tech company

Mo Chreach! Music Technology Ltd is a small company based in Scotland, set up in 2023 to make software for audio. That sounds grand, but really it's just me: I'm passionate about music and technology, and I love making things.

The channel started as a place to share songs and tutorials, and grew into long, careful guides to the instruments I use, like the OP-XY video manual, with chapters for every feature. Alongside that there are live jams, coding tutorials for audio and MIDI, and videos about the tools I build.

Mo chreach! is Scottish Gaelic, roughly "my ruin!": an old cry of dismay, and a fair description of what happens to your bank balance once you get into synths.
