Two years of OP-XY firmware is a lot to keep in your head, so here's the
[video](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y) as a list: what each update added, the buttons to use it, and a link to
the moment I show it on the device. Update from
[teenage.engineering/downloads/op-xy](https://teenage.engineering/downloads/op-xy) first.

Combos read as: hold the first thing, then press the second. "com → M1 → …" means open the com menu, press `M1`,
then pick the item.

## Winter 2024 (1.0.9 to 1.0.15)

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [New SFX and VOX samples](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=47) | New factory samples to play with. | 1.0.9 |
| [Hold a step to copy it](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=67) | Hold a step, then press an empty step. Copies notes, step components and p-locks. | 1.0.13 |
| [Scrub note timing](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=146) | Hold a step and hold `-` or `+` to slide the note smoothly. | 1.0.13 |
| [Pitch bend calibration](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=233) | com → `M1` → pitchbend sets how far the bend goes each way. | 1.0.13 |
| [Linked tracks get pitch bend](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=297) | Bend one track and its linked tracks follow. | 1.0.15 |

## Spring 2025 (1.0.25 to 1.0.38)

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|

| [Copy and paste drum keys](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=431) | Hold a key + `M2` to copy, hold another key + `M3` to paste. | 1.0.29 |
| [Sample one input channel](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=498) | `shift` + dark gray knob picks L, R or LR when sampling. | 1.0.29 |
| [Clone a scene](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=566) | `shift` + `M1` copies a scene to build the next one from. | 1.0.29 |
| [96 scenes in a song](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=681) | `shift` + the naturals picks between songs. | 1.0.29 |
| [Save as](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=778) | `shift` + `M2` saves a copy under a new name. | 1.0.32 |
| [Battery level](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=810) | Hold com and the level meter shows the battery. | 1.0.36 |
| [Count-in](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=843) | Hold record + play, then play, for a count-in before live recording. | 1.0.38 |

## Summer 2025 (1.0.45)

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [Note length: full step or overlap](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=897) | Hold a step + another step, then press the end step again to toggle. Bright LED: the full step. Dim: the length from the bar page. | 1.0.45 |
| [Drums move by semitones](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1052) | `shift` + `-` or `+` on a drum track moves notes a semitone, not an octave. | 1.0.45 |
| [Copy and paste scenes](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1124) | `shift` + `M2` to copy, `shift` + `M3` to paste. | 1.0.45 |
| [Stop at the end of a song](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1179) | Switch "loop song" off and the song stops at the end. | 1.0.45 |

## 1.1: slices, duck and sound link

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [Sample slicing](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1257) | Hold a key + `M1`. Dark gray picks transient, even or tap. In tap mode, tap `M1` to start and again to slice. | 1.1.0 |
| [Duck LFO](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1383) | `shift` + `M4`. Dark gray: source (any track or the metronome). Mid gray: amount. Light gray: hold. White: release. | 1.1.0 |
| [Sound link](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1479) | Change patterns without changing the track's sound. | 1.1.0 |
| [Queued scene changes](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1649) | `shift` + play, then an accidental, lines up the next scene to switch in time. | 1.1.0 |
| [P-locks on the samplers](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1818) | Hold a step and turn a knob, now on the drum and synth samplers too. | 1.1.0 |

## 2026: folders and 16 patterns (1.1.3 to 1.1.25)

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [Duplicate a bar, everything included](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1863) | Hold a bar + `shift` + `+`. Step components and p-locks come too. | 1.1.3 |
| [16 patterns per track](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1941) | Up from 9. | 1.1.15 |
| [Preset folders](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=1989) | `shift` + track to sort presets into folders. | 1.1.15 |
| [Samples preview as you browse](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2084) | Each sample plays as you select it. | 1.1.15 |
| [Voice and CPU warnings](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2145) | A voice count appears above 16 voices and blinks red when voices are stolen. | 1.1.15 |
| [MIDI monitor](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2307) | com → `M1` → monitor shows incoming notes, channels, values, clock and sysex. | 1.1.15 |
| [Rotate trigs](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2355) | Hold a track + `-` or `+` shifts the whole sequence a step. Notes wrap around. | 1.1.15 |
| [Quantize for odd scales](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2429) | A quantize grid for x3, x5, x6 and x7 track scales. | 1.1.25 |

## 1.2: Drumline and USB audio

| Feature | How | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [Drumline](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2533) | A drum synth engine with ten models (`shift` + `M1`). `shift` + dark gray changes the model per key, `shift` + click dark gray loops a sound. | 1.2.0 |
| [Multi-channel USB audio](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2780) | com → `M1` → usb sends up to 8 separate channels to your computer. | 1.2.0 |
| [Dynamic filter](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=2918) | `shift` + `M3`. Dark gray: high-pass. Mid gray: compress. Light gray: distort. White: low-pass. | 1.2.0 |
| [Detune](https://youtu.be/n9EemR1X34Y?t=3035) | `shift` + instrument, in the preset settings. | 1.2.0 |
