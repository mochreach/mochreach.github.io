## Privacy policy

This covers **Mo Chreach Tools**, the software Mo Chreach! Music Technology Ltd uses to manage its own
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@mochreach). It isn't offered to anyone else.

### What it accesses

When signed in, Mo Chreach Tools uses the YouTube API Services to read and update the channel's own videos:
titles, descriptions, tags, chapters, thumbnails, privacy settings and publishing schedules. It doesn't access
any other Google or YouTube data, and it doesn't access viewers' data.

### Where data is kept

The tool runs on our own computer. The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on that computer, and video
metadata is kept in our own private files. Nothing is sent to any service other than YouTube, and nothing is
sold, shared or used for advertising.

### Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from
[Google's security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the stored token removes the
tool's access too.

### YouTube and Google

By using YouTube through this tool, you agree to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
