Lattice

The keyboard hacker's audio tracker. Make music in your terminal, driven entirely from the keyboard, with a sequencer, synths and effects built from scratch.

Install Lattice

On macOS or Linux, in a terminal:

{{ sh }}

It picks the build for your computer, checks it, and puts lattice in ~/.local/bin without sudo. Run it again to update. Read the script before you pipe it to sh.

On Windows, in PowerShell:

{{ ps1 }}

It puts lattice.exe in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\lattice and adds it to your PATH. Windows is untried so far: tell me how it goes. Read the script.

With Nix, NixOS included:

nix run github:mochreach/lattice

Or add the flake's package to your system. The downloaded builds won't run on NixOS.

Then start with lattice --tutor. Every build is on the releases page.

  • All keyboard

    Keys modelled on the Helix editor: select, then act. The built-in tutor teaches them as you go, and the manual explains every one.

  • Its own instruments

    Eleven, written by hand in Rust: Krell, an alien harpsichord; Switched, a screaming modular; Monochord, a modelled string; and the Rhythmicon drums.

  • Sampling and chopping

    Play sound files, render a loop of your own song and chop it into pieces, or record over the beat with the looper.

  • Patterns that move

    Rules rotate, drift, reverse and transpose a track as it goes round; gestures and locks change a single step; the conductor shapes the tempo.

Made for an audience of one

I've always liked trackers, but the ones I used never quite matched how I think, so I made the one in my head. Every feature is there because I wanted it. It's well documented and quite usable, and I hope you get something from it, but it's made for me first. The story behind it is on the blog.

The source is free to use, change and even sell under the Deeply Personal Software Licence. The one thing it asks is that you make it yours: give what you build on it your own name and face.

Pre-alpha. Made and played on Linux; it builds and passes its tests on macOS and Windows, but I haven't played on them yet.
